A correction to the original post. While building a follow-up eval, I found that the arXiv arm of this experiment ran on misaligned example data: `examples/ai_arxiv_papers.zip` and `ai_arxiv_vectors.npy` are not in the same row order (details and a reproduction in #176), so my arXiv clusters were geometrically fine but grounded with the wrong documents. Both arms of the A/B were equally affected, so the comparison was internally consistent, but the arXiv-specific numbers (the ~23% contrast win rate, and my reading of arXiv as a "flatter" corpus) were measured on topically incoherent doc-groups and shouldn't be trusted. The 20-newsgroups results, which the headline conclusion rests on, are unaffected, and the conclusion itself also matched your experience with early versions. The label-quality work I posted above is likewise unaffected (it re-embeds the document text directly rather than using the bundled vectors). I'd rather over-flag than leave a number standing that I know is compromised.
